Basic Medical Tests & PKR Pricing

Complete Blood Count (CBC)

PKR 850

🔬 CBC Profile
Parameters: RBC, WBC, Hemoglobin, Hematocrit, Platelets, MCV, MCH, MCHC.
Clinical use: Anemia, infection, blood disorders.
⏱️ Report: 6 hours

Lipid Profile

PKR 1250

❤️ Lipid Panel
Total Cholesterol, HDL, LDL, Triglycerides, VLDL.
Cardiovascular risk assessment. Fasting required (10-12h).

Liver Function Test (LFT)

PKR 1150

🧪 Liver Panel
ALT, AST, ALP, Total Bilirubin, Direct Bilirubin, Total Protein, Albumin.
Monitor liver health & hepatitis.

Renal Function Test (RFT)

PKR 990

🩸 Kidney Profile
Urea, Creatinine, Uric Acid, BUN ratio, Electrolytes (Sodium/Potassium).
Kidney function & hydration status.

Thyroid Profile (T3,T4,TSH)

PKR 1490

🦋 Thyroid Panel
T3, T4 (Free & Total), TSH Ultrasensitive.
Thyroid disorders: Hypo/Hyperthyroidism.

Blood Glucose (Fasting/Random)

PKR 350

🍬 Glucose Test
Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS) or Random Sugar. HbA1c available on request.
Diabetes screening & monitoring.
